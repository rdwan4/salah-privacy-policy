# Privacy Policy — صلاتي

**Effective date:** October 6, 2026

---

## 1. Summary

صلاتي runs **completely offline**. The app has no internet
permission, so it physically cannot send your data anywhere. There are no ads,
no analytics, no trackers, and no accounts. Everything stays on your device.

## 2. Permissions the app uses, and why

| Permission | Why it is used |
|---|---|
| Precise / approximate location | Calculated prayer times and qibla direction for your position. Read on this device only. |
| Notifications | Shows prayer-time notifications and azan alerts you enable. |
| Exact alarms (`USE_EXACT_ALARM`, `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`) | Sounds the azan at the exact prayer minute on this device. |
| Vibrate | Gentle confirmation when the qibla points to the Kaaba, and alert vibration. |
| Run at startup (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`) | Re-schedules your azan alarms after the phone restarts. |

If you deny location, the app still works using a city you pick manually.

## 3. What data is stored on your device

- Your settings: language, selected city, theme, and alert/sound choices.
- Nothing else is written. There is no usage log, no history, and no profile.

All of this data is local and is removed automatically when you uninstall the app.

## 4. What data is collected or shared

**None.**

- The app does not transmit any data — it declares no internet permission.
- No analytics, no crash reporters, no ad SDKs, no tracking SDKs.
- No account, no email, no phone number, no identifier is requested or sent.
- Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, because nothing leaves the device.

## 5. About Google Play Services

Location is obtained through Google Play Services' on-device location API. Our
app receives the coordinates in memory only to compute prayer times and the
qibla bearing. It does not upload them, log them, or store them beyond your
manually selected city.

## 6. Azkar, prayer datasets, and azan audio

All content (Hisn al-Muslim azkar, prayer-time calculation data, azan voices)
is bundled inside the app package. Nothing is downloaded and no request is made
to any server while the app is installed.

## 7. Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any
personal information from anyone, including children.

## 8. Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will ship with the app update
and the effective date above will be revised.
